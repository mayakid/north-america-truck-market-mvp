TITLE: BTS commodity and Canadian province codes

SOURCE_TYPE: BTS
CONTENT:
BTS uses two-digit Harmonized System commodity chapters. Chapter 87 covers vehicles other than railway or tramway rolling stock and their parts and accessories, so it is the available BTS chapter-level representation for automotive parts. Current BTS code documentation lists Canadian province and territory codes used by the raw data, including XO for Ontario, XQ for Quebec, XM for Manitoba, XC for British Columbia and XA for Alberta. The two-digit chapter is broad and does not by itself establish HazMat handling requirements.
