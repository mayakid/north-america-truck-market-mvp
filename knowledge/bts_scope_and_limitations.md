EVIDENCE_ID: BTS-SCOPE-LIMITATIONS
TITLE: Scope and interpretation of BTS TransBorder Freight Data
SOURCE_URL: https://www.bts.gov/statistical-products/transborder-freight-data/north-american-transborder-freight-data-faqs
SOURCE_TYPE: BTS
CONTENT:
BTS TransBorder Freight Data are monthly administrative trade statistics derived from official U.S. import and export records. The truck mode identifies the mode used when a shipment enters or leaves the United States, not every mode used during the full journey. Canadian province fields can reflect province of clearance or reported origin and may not always be the final physical destination. Port fields are customs reporting locations and may not always equal the physical crossing point. The dataset does not provide live trucking rates, live border waiting times, carrier fleet size or carrier HazMat qualifications.
