TITLE: BTS 2025 TransBorder annual report truck-market context
SOURCE_URL: https://www.bts.gov/newsroom/transborder-freight-data-annual-report-2025-0
SOURCE_TYPE: BTS
CONTENT:
The BTS 2025 annual report identifies trucking as the largest mode for U.S.-Canada freight by value. It also identifies Detroit, Port Huron and Buffalo-Niagara Falls as the largest U.S.-Canada truck ports in that annual aggregate. These are national annual context statistics; they are not a commodity-specific province recommendation and must not be presented as a joint state + commodity + province + port estimate.
